## 1. What we sell

We sell a convenient, repeatable label and sticker service — not merely a design app.

**Customer promise:** Choose a template, add your product details, send us the preview, approve the production proof, and receive printed, cut and packaged stickers.

The service removes the difficult parts: design setup, dimensions, print preparation, cutting, packing, repeat orders and delivery coordination.

## 2. Initial customer segments

Prioritise businesses that regularly package physical products:

1. Food, snacks, spices, sauces and condiments.
2. Cosmetics, skincare, haircare, soap and detergents.
3. Drinks, zobo, juices, water and beverages.
4. Honey, oils, agricultural products and packaged farm produce.
5. Bakers, caterers and confectionery businesses.
6. Perfume, candles and home-care products.
7. Small manufacturers and distributors.

Start with owner-operated businesses that can be contacted through their public business pages or a warm introduction.

## 3. Offer and order policy

- Standard MOQ: 100 pieces.
- Trial quantities: 20 or 50 pieces only when the material, size and production setup make them viable; quote individually.
- Bulk orders: 1,000, 2,000, 5,000, 20,000 and larger quantities receive a custom quote.
- New-customer sample: 10 printed stickers for an eligible business, subject to approved artwork and available sample material.
- Pickup: offered where the production partner's arrangement permits.
- Delivery: nationwide delivery can be arranged; price and ETA are confirmed by destination and parcel size.
- Rush orders: accepted only after production capacity and the real rush fee are confirmed.
- Reorders: retain the approved artwork/specification and make repeat ordering simple.

The free 10-piece sample is a customer-acquisition cost. Track its print, cut, packaging and delivery cost separately. Do not promise free nationwide courier delivery on every sample.

## 4. Public pricing — approval gate

Do not publish a final price list until Sleekblue supplies written wholesale/production costs for each defined specification.

For a comparable small product-label order, public Nigerian listings currently show examples such as:
- InkSnap: ₦15,000 per 100 in its published quantity bands.
- Pziel: ₦18,450 for 100 standard product labels (listed 2.5 × 3 inches).
- MyPrinta: ₦20,000 for 100 vinyl stickers, with lower per-piece prices at higher quantities.
- Hazken: examples from ₦11,400 for 100 square product labels and ₦18,450 for 100 standard product labels.

These are market references, not directly comparable specifications and not our wholesale costs. Materials, size, adhesive, finishing, cut type, VAT and delivery differ. See:
- https://www.inksnapng.com/sticker-printing.html
- https://www.pzielng.com/label-sticker-printing
- https://myprinta.ng/prices/stickers
- https://hazken.com/sticker-print

### Required cost sheet from Sleekblue

For each common product specification, obtain:
- material and adhesive type;
- standard sizes and custom-size charging method;
- print cost per sheet / square metre / unit;
- cutting and finishing cost;
- setup or plate charge, if any;
- minimum viable run;
- spoilage allowance;
- production lead time and rush surcharge;
- packaging cost;
- pickup arrangements;
- delivery handoff and who bears courier cost.

### Retail price formula

Retail price = production cost + packaging + payment fees + delivery subsidy (if any) + customer reward allowance + target gross profit.

Use quantity tiers only after the cost model is verified. Keep delivery separate unless a specific promotion includes it. The website must show a quote before payment; never use unapproved default prices.

## 5. Rewards and referrals — margin-protected

Do not issue cash-like credits before the production margin is known.

Recommended pilot rules:
- Award credits only after an order is paid and marked fulfilled.
- Calculate the reward from collected eligible print revenue, excluding delivery, taxes, refunds and pass-through ad spend.
- Cap total reward cost to a small, approved share of the order's contribution margin.
- Credits are non-transferable, non-cash, have visible expiry and can only be redeemed for listed services.
- Show the customer the credit amount, value, expiry and eligible use.

### Advertising credits

Credits may pay for our approved ad setup or campaign-management service. Meta advertising spend is separate and must be funded by the customer in their own Meta account. Do not describe service credits as free ad spend.

### Referral commissions

- Create a unique referral link/code for each approved referrer.
- Attribute the referred business to the first valid referral.
- Pay commission only after the referred customer's qualifying order has been paid and fulfilled.
- Calculate commission from contribution margin, not gross order value.
- Publish the rate, payout timing, minimum payout and refund/clawback rules before inviting referrers.

## 6. Production and order workflow

1. Customer designs a label using a template.
2. Customer saves a private draft by default.
3. Customer submits a preview and order details.
4. The system creates a reference number and sends the request to the business WhatsApp inbox.
5. Staff confirm the artwork, exact dimensions, material, finish, cut path, quantity, delivery method and final price.
6. Customer approves the final proof and pays using Paystack or an approved transfer method.
7. Staff mark the order paid and send a production ticket to Sleekblue.
8. Sleekblue prints, cuts and packs the job.
9. Staff quality-check quantity, colour, cut, adhesion/finish and packaging.
10. Customer receives pickup or delivery details; order status is updated.
11. Approved artwork/specifications are retained for repeat orders under the stated retention policy.

**Never send a browser preview directly to print.** The final artwork must be checked for physical dimensions, bleed, safe area, cut path, fonts/images and the production partner's actual CMYK/profile requirements. A browser RGB preview is not proof of CMYK print readiness.

## 7. Customer trust and privacy

- Make the business identity, WhatsApp number, sample terms, pricing conditions and delivery process visible.
- Default customer designs to private.
- Public gallery publication requires an explicit choice and a working server-side permission check.
- Never expose private artwork through guessable URLs or public object-storage folders.
- Let customers understand what is saved, for how long and who can access it.
- Provide a way to request deletion, subject to legitimate order/record-keeping requirements.
- Do not claim that a design is cloud-saved, publicly published or permanently attached to a customer account until the backend actually supports it.

## 8. First 100 customers

### Prospecting groups
- 30 food / beverage brands.
- 25 beauty / personal-care businesses.
- 20 bakeries / caterers / snack makers.
- 15 honey / agriculture / household-product sellers.
- 10 small manufacturers / distributors.

### Outreach sequence
1. Find a real business with a visible packaged product.
2. Personalise the message using its business/product name.
3. Offer a free label-design link and 10-piece sample subject to the stated terms.
4. Share a short screen recording showing how to choose a template and change the label.
5. Ask one easy question: "Would you like me to make a sample label for one of your products?"
6. Follow up once after 3–5 days if there is no reply.
7. Stop immediately on opt-out or objection.
8. Record replies, samples requested, samples fulfilled, quotes, paid orders and repeat orders separately.

Do not mass-message scraped lists. Do not use fake customer counts, fake reviews, fake live activity or false scarcity.

## 9. Launch metrics

Track weekly:
- qualified businesses contacted;
- human replies;
- sample requests;
- sample production cost;
- quotes issued;
- paid orders;
- average order value;
- gross profit / contribution margin by job;
- on-time fulfilment;
- reorders;
- referral orders;
- delivery cost per order;
- complaints, remakes and refunds.

The goal is not just 100 sign-ups. It is 100 real businesses, with a meaningful share placing a paid order and returning.
